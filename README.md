I work on [DanNet](https://github.com/kuhumcst/DanNet), the Danish WordNet, at
the University of Copenhagen. In my spare time I write Clojure(Script)
libraries, mostly implementations of open standards. I also keep curated lists
of Clojure resources for
[graph-like data](https://github.com/simongray/clojure-graph-resources) and
[DSLs](https://github.com/simongray/clojure-dsl-resources).

My blog, [simon.grays.blog](https://simon.grays.blog), runs on
[indieblog](https://github.com/simongray/indieblog) and the IndieWeb libraries
below.
